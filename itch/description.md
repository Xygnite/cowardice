# Cowardice

**A dice roguelite where you live by the block.**

You get three dice, two rerolls a turn and 40 HP. Every turn you throw, keep what you like and put each die into a slot: **Attack** to hit, **Guard** to soak damage, or **Skill** to fire a combo. When the enemy swings, you press SPACE at the right moment. A good block cuts the damage, a perfect block cancels it, and a timed parry beats attacks that would otherwise go straight through you.

### Roll, place, react
- **Six combo skills** are built from dice patterns. Two matching dice make a Cleave, three of a kind make a Sunder, a straight like 2-3-4 makes a Flurry, and a 1 and a 6 make a Gambit. Three low dice make a **Cower**, which makes every enemy attack that turn miss. Sometimes running away is the right call.
- **Every enemy shows its next move.** Some guard, some curse your dice, and some ring a bell that lets another enemy hit you with an attack you can't block.
- **Leaving a die unplaced gives you Grit**, which adds a reroll next turn.

### Forge your dice
Enemies drop Shards. At the Forge you spend 4 Shards to throw a die at the anvil. What it lands on is what you get, and you choose which face to put it on:
- **+1** or **+4** to the face's number
- **Sharp**: ignores enemy guard
- **Ember**: heals when the die is placed
- **Ward**: counts double in Guard
- **Shard**: pays out more Shards

A mark only works when its face comes up, so as the run goes on, the dice turn into your build.

### Relics: make the run snowball
Win a fight and you pick one of three **relics** for your belt. Every hit is its base damage times **Mult**, and relics are how Mult grows: Whetstone sharpens your high rolls, Spare Fuse rewards the rerolls you don't use, Gravedigger's Tally grows with every kill, and Pit Lantern doubles everything if you go all in on Attack. There are 16 relics and 5 belt slots, so a full belt means breaking one to take another. The order matters too: a Mirror Shard copies the relic to its right.

### Pick your route
Each floor is a new branching map. Choose your path past fights, campfires, forges and **"?" rooms**: short events with a trade-off, often decided by a roll of your own dice. Some of them are ambushes.

### Two floors, two bosses
- **The Sunken Chapel** has bone rats, shield acolytes, a plague doctor, Mother Moss and her curses, and the **Bone Warden** waiting at the bottom.
- **The Hollow Mine** lies beneath the Chapel, where a dead mining crew still digs. Tunnel crawlers hit harder in packs, a Rubble Hulk breaks into more rubble when it dies, and the **Pit King** buries your dice under cave-ins.

### Between runs
Each run earns **Embers**, win or lose. Spend them at camp to unlock skills and 10 perks such as Steady Hand, Iron Nerve, Hex Ward and Gambler's Heart. You can equip two perks per run.

**Features**
- A guided tutorial that spotlights one thing at a time
- Hand-drawn, animated pixel art for every character and backdrop
- Save at a campfire and continue later
- Play in your browser, or download the Windows exe (a single file with nothing to install)

**Controls**
- **Hold R** (or the Roll button) to wind up a throw, then let go
- **Click a die** to move it to the next slot, **right-click** to move it back, **hold** to lock it. If you prefer, switch Dice control to Menu in Settings to pick from a menu instead.
- **SPACE** or **left click** on the tray to block and parry · **Enter** to end the turn · **Esc** for the menu · **M** to mute
