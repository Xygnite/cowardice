# Devlog #2: Relics, a guided tutorial, and you can play it in your browser (v0.1.1)

Devlog #1 ended on "relics are next." They're in. Version 0.1.1 also brings a new tutorial, tougher bosses, and a build you can play right on this page.

## Relics: a run that snowballs

Win a fight and you get to pick one of three **relics**. They last for the rest of the run and sit on a **belt** next to the dice tray. There are 16 of them: 9 common and 7 rare, each with its own pixel icon.

Most relics raise your **Mult**. Every hit is now its base damage times Mult. Mult starts at 1, relics add to it, and the rare ones multiply it. A few examples:

- **Whetstone**: Attack dice showing 5 or 6 deal +3 each
- **Twin Saints**: when Cleave or Sunder fires, +1 Mult
- **Spare Fuse**: each reroll you don't use adds +0.25 Mult for the rest of the fight
- **Gravedigger's Tally**: +0.1 Mult for every kill since you took it, and it keeps growing
- **Hungry Bell** (rare): +1 Mult for each turn in a row that a skill fires
- **Pit Lantern** (rare): x2 Mult when every placed die is in Attack
- **Mirror Shard** (rare): copies the relic to its right

The belt holds five relics. Once it's full, taking a new one means breaking an old one, which pays 2 Shards. Order matters too: move relics left and right to set up what the Mirror copies.

Beating the Bone Warden opens his hoard, a pick from rares only. Starting a run in the Mine gives you a rare pick as well.

The fight screen shows your Mult next to your damage. Hover a relic to read what it does. Relics flash gold when they count as the blow lands. The end-of-run screen lists your relics, your highest Mult and your biggest hit.

## Bosses that can take a hit

With relics, a good run hits very hard, so the late game had to catch up. Fights deeper into a floor have more HP, the **Bone Warden** now has 120 HP, and the **Pit King** has 220.

## A tutorial that shows you

The old tutorial was a wall of text above the fight. The new one goes one step at a time. The screen darkens apart from a **spotlight** on the thing being explained, such as the enemy's intent, a die, a slot or the Roll button, and a panel tells you what it does. Only the highlighted thing responds, so you can't get lost. The dice in each tutorial turn are set in advance, so every lesson shows exactly what it's meant to. The block and parry bars wait while their step is explained.

## Smaller things

- **The dice stay in your hand** until the turn's first throw. The tray no longer shows last turn's dice while it waits.
- **The map has a Menu** (button or Esc). Quitting from it gives up the run.
- **The menu opens mid-fight** while a throw is waiting, and closing it leaves the throw where it was.

## Play in your browser

Cowardice now runs right here on itch.io, with nothing to download. Progress saves in your browser, so come back in the same browser and your Embers, perks and campfire saves are there. (A private window or clearing site data starts fresh.)

The Windows exe is still here if you'd rather download it, and it now shows its version, 0.1.1, in its file properties.

## This build

- Two floors: the Sunken Chapel and the Hollow Mine
- 15 enemies and 2 bosses
- **16 relics** and a five-slot belt
- Branching maps with 9 "?" events, forges and campfires
- Timed block and parry
- 6 combo skills
- A forge throw for face marks: Sharp, Ember, Ward, Shard
- 10 perks to unlock between runs
- A guided tutorial
- Campfire saves
- Play in the browser, or download the Windows exe
